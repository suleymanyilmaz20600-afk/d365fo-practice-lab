# D365FO Practice Lab Roadmap

## Faz 1 — Shell + navigation
Tamamlandı.

## Faz 2 — Finance işlem pratiği
GL journal validate/post/voucher, AP invoice/payment/settlement, AR invoice/payment/settlement, posting profile, VAT, exchange-rate ve FX farkı pratikleri.

## Faz 3 — Production troubleshooting lab
Yanlış posting, closed period, missing dimension, settlement mismatch, bank reconciliation, FX revaluation, fixed asset, budget, security ve import ticketları.

## Faz 4 — SCM junior transaction engine
Product → PO → receipt → invoice; inventory movement/counting/transfer; sales order → packing slip → invoice; warehouse work; planning; costing ve Finance bağlantısı.

## Faz 5 — X++ developer lab
Syntax/OOP, tables/EDTs/enums, queries, joins, transactions, CoC/events, SysOperation/batch, entities/OData, performance, testing ve code review.

## Faz 6 — Capstone
Finance + SCM + X++ uçtan uca şirket senaryosu, production ticketları, UAT/cutover ve handover.
