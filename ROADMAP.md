# D365FO Practice Lab Roadmap

## Faz 1 — Shell + navigation
Tamamlandı.

## Faz 2 — Finance işlem pratiği
Tamamlandı. GL journal validate/post/voucher, AP invoice/payment/settlement, AR invoice/payment/settlement, posting profile, VAT, exchange-rate ve FX farkı pratikleri.

## Faz 3 — Production troubleshooting lab
Tamamlandı. Yanlış posting, closed period, missing dimension, settlement mismatch, bank reconciliation, FX revaluation, fixed asset, budget, security ve import ticketları.

## Faz 4 — SCM junior transaction engine
Tamamlandı. Product master → PO confirmation → partial/full product receipt → vendor invoice → physical/financial inventory → transfer/counting → sales reservation → packing slip → invoice → planning → planned-order firming → costing/Finance bridge.

## Faz 5 — X++ developer lab
Tamamlandı. Syntax/OOP, AOT/model/package, table/EDT/enum, query/join ve N+1, transactions/forUpdate/ttsLevel, set-based performance, extension-first/CoC, SysOperation/batch, Data Entity/OData, security-aware development, unit testing, code review/performance clinic ve techno-functional capstone.

## Faz 6 — Capstone
Sıradaki faz. Finance + SCM + X++ uçtan uca şirket senaryosu: şirket setup, master data, P2P/O2C, closing, troubleshooting, requirement/fit-gap, standard-vs-custom, X++ task, UAT, cutover, production incident ve handover.
